The new Sinima, built from your picks

Five screens, each at desktop and phone size, in the structure you approved. Under every screen is the list of your picks it follows and what was taken from each. Everything shown is real: the cinemas, films and times are from the Lagos listing saved on 11 November, with the clock at 13:45. This is the first design again, now with the motion from the second: parts rise in one after another as a screen scrolls into view, cards and times react when you hover, panels slide, and the share card pops. Press Replay all motion in the bar above, or Replay on any frame. The colours are unchanged for now; the yellow is next.

When you have a font you like, press the button. It copies one line to paste back to Claude; add what you want changed after it.
