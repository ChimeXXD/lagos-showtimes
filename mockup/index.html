Sinima · home redesign, draft 2

The same layout, in the style of cofounder.co

The five screens you approved the layout of, redrawn with that site as the hard reference. Its colours, type, shapes and motion were measured from the live site, not guessed. Everything shown is real: the cinemas, films and times are from the Lagos listing saved on 11 November, with the clock at 13:45. Hover over cards, chips and times; scroll to see each screen arrive; press Replay on any frame.

ColourA warm off-white page, near-black ink, greys made from the ink, and hairlines at 10%. Colour only appears in pictures and one small green dot.
TypeHeadings at normal weight in two tones: the point in ink, the rest in grey. Small labels in a mono font, like their “1.0 — LAUNCH”. Their font, TT Neoris, is paid; try the six free ones above.
ShapeButtons and chips with 8 px corners, not capsules. Cards with 12 px corners, a thin ring and a soft shadow. The dark button has their slight inner highlight.
MotionParts rise 8 px out of a slight blur over 0.42 s, one after another. Hover changes take 0.15 s. Panels slide on a long, soft ease. The share card pops with a small overshoot. All off for people who ask for less motion.

When you have a font you like, press the button. It copies one line to paste back to Claude; add what you want changed after it.
