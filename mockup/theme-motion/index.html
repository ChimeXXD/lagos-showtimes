Theme switch: four ways to move

The circle was your portfolio's, so here are four that belong to a cinema. Each plays on the real Cinemas tab, light to dark and back, on a loop. The switch's icon changes too: the sun sets as the moon rises, so it no longer turns. Press Quarter speed to see each step. With "reduce motion" on, every option becomes a plain cross-fade.

Pick one, then press Copy and paste the line to Claude, with anything you'd change.
