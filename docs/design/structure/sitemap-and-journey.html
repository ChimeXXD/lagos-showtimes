01 · The idea

Start from the cinema, not from a list of times

What you asked for, as I understand it: you open Sinima and see the cinemas near you. You pick one and see what it is showing, as posters, by day. You tap a time and book on that cinema's own site.

Before · what is live now

One long list of times

  • Home is a single column of showings sorted by start time.
  • Your area hides behind a button that opens a list.
  • One showing per row, on desktop too. Posters are empty boxes.
  • No way to start from a cinema you already like.
After · this proposal

Cinemas first, films as posters, three ways in

  • Home shows cinemas, nearest your area first. Your area is on the page, not in a dropdown.
  • A cinema's films are poster cards in a grid: several per row on desktop, two per row on a phone.
  • Every film has artwork. No empty boxes.
  • Two other ways in for people who think differently: by film, or by what starts soon.
1

Cinemas

"I go to Filmhouse Lekki. What's on?" The default. Pick a cinema, see its films by day.

2

Films

"I want to see Clayface. Where and when?" Pick a film, see every cinema showing it.

3

Starting soon

"I'm free now. What can I catch?" Films starting in the next 2 hours near you.

Notices that can appear on any screen

"Sample data" while the site runs on saved pages. "Some times were read yesterday" when a source is a day old. "You're offline" with the age of the saved times. One notice at a time, the most serious one.

Not in this structure

Accounts, ticket sales, seat maps, ads, push notifications. A week view: most cinemas publish only today and tomorrow, so the day tabs show as many days as the data holds.

Next time you open it

Sinima remembers the area on your phone and opens with it set. Nothing is sent anywhere.

"Nearest" means area, for now

We know each cinema's area, not its exact position on a map. So the order is: your area, the areas next to it, then the rest. No distances or travel times are shown, because we could not stand behind them.

Use my location

Only when you tap it. The phone works out the closest area and Sinima keeps just that area's name. If you say no: "No problem. Pick your area."

On desktop

The same chips sit in a row under the header. The cinema cards and posters form a grid of several per row instead of a single column.

06 · The Book button

Where Book takes you, case by case

"Directed to the correct cinema site" depends on what each cinema publishes. Sinima only ever uses the link that came with the showing, checked to be on that cinema's own site or the ticketing service it uses.

What the data can and can't say today

In the Lagos listing saved on 11 Nov, only the 7 Filmhouse cinemas say the format of each showing (IMAX at Lekki IMAX and Circle Mall, MX4D at Landmark). The other 13 cinemas give no format at all. With a format chosen they are left out and counted: "13 cinemas don't say their format". We never guess one.

Where the format shows

A tag on poster cards, on each time, in the showing panel and on the share card. On the Cinemas tab, a cinema shows the formats it has ("IMAX · 4D") and the same chips filter the list.

Films not out yet

Cinemas publish about two days ahead, so a film can be found once it has a showing. A "coming soon" list is not in this structure; say if you want it added.

09 · Approved

Six decisions, approved on 12 November

You approved all six. They are kept here as the record. New in this draft: the format journey on the page before this one.

1

Home is Cinemas

Opening Sinima shows cinemas, nearest your area first. Films and Starting soon are the other two tabs. My recommendation: yes.

2

Area as chips on the page

Your area is always visible and changes with one tap. No dropdown and no sheet. "Use my location" is offered once, at the top.

3

A time opens a panel, not the cinema's site

Tapping a time shows the showing first (freshness, countdown, Remind me, Share), with Book as the main button. The other option is for a time to open the cinema's site at once, which is one tap shorter but skips all of that.

4

Days, not weeks

Day tabs show as many days as each cinema has published, usually today and tomorrow. A week view would be mostly empty.

5

Starting soon becomes a tab

It was the whole home page and the app's one-line pitch. Here it is one of three ways in. If you would rather it stayed on the home page as a strip above the cinemas, say so.

6

Cinemas we can't read are still listed

Silverbird Galleria and Leisure Mall have no times in our source. They appear as "Times not available yet" with a link to their own site.

After your yes

A reference board for the new structure, a font page with free fonts close to the one on cofounder.co, and Danfo applied to real screens.

Then

Full designs of each screen, desktop and phone side by side, for you to pick from. Nothing is built until you say "go with it".

About this document

The small screens are diagrams of structure, not designs. The colours are Danfo, as a first test. The font is a stand-in.
