## Surfaces and searches

| Starting soon list | Time-first lists that scan fast | Transit departure boards, Citymapper, train apps, flight status, cinema chain apps' "today" lists, Letterboxd showtimes |

## Pattern summary

- **What the picks share:**
  - Light pages, black text, lots of white. The four-tick picks (Luma, Meetup, Google TV, Apple TV, Hypelist, Sweatpals, Luma calendar, Meetup calendar, Fixtured) are all calm and roomy, with colour used once.
  - Rounded shapes: capsule buttons, soft cards, outlined badges. Chime named Peacock's font and buttons, and Fixtured's share sheet.
  - A rounded, geometric typeface (Peacock, Fixtured, Meetup) rather than a plain system look.
  - Film artwork is welcome: Hypelist, Apple TV, Google TV and Peacock are all poster-led.
  - Quiet grey "when" lines for freshness (Untitled, Yahoo Finance, Dropbox), never a strip or a badge.
  - A small drawing in the empty state (Uber). Chime wants custom illustrations and a custom icon.
- **What to avoid:**
  - One loud colour filling a block (all four MUBI GO screens, InPost's yellow, IMDb's coloured score square).
  - Dense tables and tight rows (Expedia, Shopee's film page, MLS's boxed times).
  - Dark-first, oversized headings (Open).
  - Rows that are too bare (Tock) and warning strips pinned to the screen edge (Brex).
  - Distances in the area picker (Grab).
- **Open questions for the direction board:**
  - List, timeline rail or poster rows for "Starting soon"?
  - Do posters earn their data cost on every row, or only on the film page?
  - How loud is Stay or Go: capsule, tile or small badge?
  - A loaded typeface (30 KB limit) or the phone's own font?
  - Illustration style and the app icon: reference pass still to do (O-13).
