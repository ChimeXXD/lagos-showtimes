## Surfaces and searches

| Surface | Look for | Search ideas |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting soon list | Time-first lists that scan fast | Transit departure boards, Citymapper, train apps, flight status, cinema chain apps' "today" lists, Letterboxd showtimes |
| Freshness and source | Quiet "updated" lines, provenance | Weather apps ("Updated 5 min ago"), news apps, Lagocine's "last read", Meridian's wording |
| Countdown | A clear "starts in" without anxiety | Ride-hailing ETAs, sports kick-off countdowns, Apple Sports |
| Credits chip | A small answer with a source | RunPee "Anything Extra", nutrition badges, review site badges |
| Area picker | City areas, not maps | Food delivery address pickers (Chowdeck, Glovo), Bolt, Jumia |
| Film page | Every showing across venues | Fandango, Odeon, Cineworld, Vue, BookMyShow |
| Empty and offline states | Warm, useful, light | Google Maps offline, YouTube offline, Opera Mini |
| Share card | WhatsApp link previews that read well small | Event links, Spotify share cards |
